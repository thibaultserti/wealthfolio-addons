# Assets Performance Addon for Wealthfolio

Compare individual asset performance (IRR, TWR), explore multi-asset correlation matrices, and benchmark against major market indices (S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, MSCI World) and your overall portfolio.

## Features

- **Individual Asset IRR & TWR**: Accurate calculations of Money-Weighted Returns (IRR / XIRR) with cash flow dates and Time-Weighted Returns (TWR).
- **Multi-Asset Correlation Matrix**: Interactive heatmap displaying Pearson correlation coefficients based on daily return series across customizable time horizons.
- **Benchmark & Portfolio Comparison**: Compare assets against market benchmarks (S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, etc.) and against your overall portfolio with Alpha, Beta, Sharpe ratio, and Drawdown metrics.
- **Interactive Multi-Asset Comparison Chart**: Rebased normalized returns chart (1M, 3M, 6M, YTD, 1Y, 3Y, 5Y, ALL).
- **Asset Deep Dive**: Individual asset view with full transaction cash flow breakdown and comparative statistics.
